# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on technical outcomes, not individuals.
- Use inclusive language.
- Collaborate in good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate disruption.
- Doxxing or publication of private information.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized language.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.

## Reporting

Do not report conduct issues in public issues or pull requests.

Use a private channel to contact maintainers directly. Include:

- what happened
- where it happened (link)
- when it happened
- any supporting evidence

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any appropriate corrective action, including:

- warning
- temporary suspension
- permanent ban

Maintainers may remove or reject content that violates this Code of Conduct.
